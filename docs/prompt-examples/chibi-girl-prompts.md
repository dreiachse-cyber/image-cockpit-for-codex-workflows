# Chibi Girl Pixel-Art Prompt Examples

作成日: 2026-07-27

このファイルは、外見、髪型、肌色、服装が明確に異なる「デフォルメ女の子」向けprompt例をまとめた参照用メモです。
2頭身の全身・中央配置を基本とし、アニメーション素材へ展開しやすい条件を揃えています。

## 共通方針

- 1枚につき1人のデフォルメ女の子のみ生成する。
- 頭、髪、手、小物、服の裾、足先まで全身を画像内に収める。
- 2頭身の中央idle poseとし、animation-readyな手足の分離と読みやすいシルエットを保つ。
- 背景は透明を優先し、不可なら完全な単色クロマキー背景を使う。

## Common Negative Prompt

```text
blur, text, watermark, logo, cropped head, cropped hair, cropped hands, cropped feet, cut off body, extra limbs, missing limbs, malformed hands, duplicate character, multiple characters, adult proportions, realistic anatomy, scenery, detailed background, floor shadow, photorealistic, 3d render, vector art
```

## Chibi Girl Prompts

### 01. Auburn-Pigtail Farmer / 赤茶ツインテールの農家少女

```text
Create exactly one character only as a single full-body two-head-tall chibi pixel-art girl asset: a fair-skinned girl with rosy freckles, auburn hair in two high fluffy pigtails, curled bangs, and warm brown eyes. Dress her in a small straw sunhat, a sky-blue denim pinafore dress over a cream gingham blouse, rust-colored knee socks, sturdy tan lace-up boots, and a tiny seed pouch at her waist, holding no tool. Give her a sunny dependable expression and a centered front-facing idle stance with separated arms and legs, clear feet contact, a readable silhouette, and animation-ready joints. Use transparent background preferred, crisp 32-bit fantasy RPG pixel-art rendering, no crops, no scenery, no readable text, no logo, and no watermark. Keep the entire hat, pigtails, head, hands, pouch, dress hem, boots, and feet fully inside the image with comfortable transparent padding. If transparency is unavailable, use a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor plane, or lighting variation.
```
